Sounds

Sound Goals
- Add urgency and outcome clarity.

Required Sound Events
- Match found
- Countdown start
- Invalid submit
- Valid submit accepted
- Win
- Loss
- Draw
- Opponent disconnected

Audio Style
- Clean, synthetic, modern.
- Short and low-latency.
- No long musical phrases in core loop.

Mixing Rules
- Timer and feedback sounds must not mask each other.
- Win/lose stings should be short enough to support quick rematch flow.
