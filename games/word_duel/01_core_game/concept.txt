Concept

Word Duel is a competitive word sprint built around two player-selected constraints:
- one required starting letter
- one required ending letter

Constraint Generation
- Each player selects one letter before the round.
- The game then randomly decides which selected letter is the required start and which is the required end.
- This hidden-direction reveal adds suspense without changing the core validation rule.

The tension comes from three simultaneous pressures:
- speed: submit before the opponent
- accuracy: invalid words do not win
- adaptability: the letter pair changes each round

Player Fantasy
- The player should feel clever, quick, and slightly under pressure.
- Winning should feel earned through recall and speed, not randomness alone.

Design Identity
- Minimalist, readable, decisive.
- Short rounds with immediate emotional payoff.
- Skill is primarily vocabulary recall plus composure under time pressure.

Why The Concept Works
- Rules are understandable in seconds.
- Inputs are simple enough for mobile.
- Every round creates a new micro-puzzle.
- Multiplayer tension is naturally high because only one correct answer is needed.

Core Success Criteria
- A new player understands the goal in under 30 seconds.
- Match resolution is usually under 20 seconds even though the timer allows 60.
- Invalid submission handling is clear and not frustrating.
- Rematch rate is high because rounds are short and outcomes are decisive.
