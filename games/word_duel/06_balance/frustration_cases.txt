Frustration Cases

High-Risk Frustrations
- "I typed a valid word and still lost."
- "The game rejected a normal word."
- "The letters were impossible."
- "The timer ended while my word was still checking."
- "My opponent disconnected and I still had to wait too long."

Mitigation Rules
- Always explain why a submission failed.
- Preserve pending validation after timer end.
- Use authoritative consistent dictionary logic.
- Limit impossible letter pairs.
- Keep disconnect resolution fast and visible.

Product Requirement
- The game should rarely leave the player unsure whether the result was fair.
