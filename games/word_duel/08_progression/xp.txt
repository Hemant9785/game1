XP

XP Purpose
- Give players a reason to continue after individual wins and losses.

Suggested XP Model
- Match completed: small base XP
- Win: significant bonus XP
- Draw: medium XP between win and loss
- Fast win: small bonus
- Clean round with no invalid attempts: small bonus

XP Design Rules
- XP should reward activity and skill without overshadowing the core duel.
- Progress should feel steady in short sessions.

Exploit Prevention
- Rematch farming against the same opponent may need diminishing returns in future if abuse appears.
