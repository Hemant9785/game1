Reward Balance

Reward Goals
- Encourage rematches and continued play.
- Reward improvement, not only outright wins.

MVP Reward Principles
- Winning should matter most.
- Losing should still provide small participation XP to reduce churn.
- Clean play and speed can provide small bonuses.

Avoid
- Large rewards for obscure long words that distort the simple race objective.
- Punishing zero-reward losses that make the game feel harsh.

Suggested Reward Signals
- Win bonus
- First valid submission bonus
- Fast finish bonus
- Daily play bonus
