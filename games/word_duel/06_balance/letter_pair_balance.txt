Letter Pair Balance

Balance Goal
- Preserve variety without creating impossible or near-impossible rounds.

Easy Pairs
- Common consonant-to-common consonant or vowel-ending combinations.
- Examples likely include C-T, B-R, S-T, P-N.

Hard Pairs
- Rare starts and rare ends such as Q-X or J-Q.

MVP Policy
- Allow some challenging letters, but avoid combinations with extremely low valid-word coverage.
- Maintain a reviewed blacklist or weight table informed by real dictionary coverage.
- Balance review must consider both directional outcomes for the same chosen pair because either mapping may be rolled at round start.

Auto-Pick Fairness
- If auto-assignment is used, prefer medium-difficulty letters.
- Do not auto-pick rare punitive pairs for first-time players.

Monitoring Metrics
- Timeout rate by pair
- Invalid-attempt rate by pair
- Average time-to-first-valid by pair
