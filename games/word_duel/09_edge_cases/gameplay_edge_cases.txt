Gameplay Edge Cases

Cases To Handle
- Player submits exactly as timer hits zero
- Winner found while opponent is mid-typing
- Both players never submit
- Both players submit only invalid words
- Auto-selected letter pair proves unwinnable
- Opponent wins during your pending validation

Rules
- Pre-expiry received submissions remain eligible.
- Round ends immediately after winner lock.
- No valid words before expiry results in draw unless a pending pre-expiry submission later validates successfully.
- If an auto-selected pair is confirmed impossible due to service mistake, round should be voided as no contest and excluded from ranked-like records.
