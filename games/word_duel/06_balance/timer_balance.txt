Timer Balance

MVP Timer
- 60 seconds per round.

Why 60 Seconds
- Long enough for search and retry.
- Short enough to preserve urgency.
- Fits mobile quick-session expectations.

Signs Timer Is Too Short
- High draw rate from ordinary pairs.
- Players often submit only once.
- New players churn after repeated time pressure failure.

Signs Timer Is Too Long
- Too many rounds feel decided early but drag to completion because no one submits.
- Low tension in mid-round.

Future Tuning
- Event modes may use 30 or 45 seconds.
- Ranked mode may keep 60 seconds for consistency.
