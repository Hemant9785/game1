Ranked Mode

Why Ranked
- Word Duel has a clear winner per round and naturally supports ladders.

Requirements Before Launch
- Stronger anti-cheat confidence
- Letter-pair fairness data
- Stable validation latency
- Disconnect abuse protections
- Match quality and skill-rating system

Ranked Rules Suggestions
- Standardized timer and letter pool
- No experimental dictionary policies
- Clear penalties for repeated disconnects
