Difficulty

Difficulty Sources
- Letter rarity
- Word familiarity
- Typing speed requirement
- Validation strictness

MVP Difficulty Target
- Most rounds should feel solvable by ordinary English speakers within 60 seconds.
- Not every player should succeed every round, but unwinnable rounds should be rare.

Difficulty Controls
- Weighted letter selection support
- Impossible-pair blacklist
- Tutorial with easy pairs
- Gradual exposure to harder letters

Bad Difficulty
- Players frequently timing out because pairs are too obscure.
- Players losing because dictionary acceptance is inconsistent.
- Rounds decided mostly by random letter cruelty rather than word recall skill.
