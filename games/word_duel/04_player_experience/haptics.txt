Haptics

Design Goal
- Reinforce urgency and outcome without turning the game noisy or tiring.

Recommended Haptic Events
- Letter confirmed: light tap
- Invalid submission: short crisp bump
- Valid submission accepted: stronger success pulse
- Round win: short celebratory sequence
- Round loss: single firm pulse
- Countdown final 3 seconds: optional subtle taps each second if tested positively

Guidelines
- Keep haptics optional through device settings respect.
- Do not fire heavy haptics on every keystroke.
- Avoid overlapping haptics with rapid spam submissions.
