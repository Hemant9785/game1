Theme

Theme Direction
- Fast, modern, focused.
- Minimalist with high-contrast surfaces and strong accent color.

Emotional Targets
- Lobby: anticipation
- Gameplay: pressure and clarity
- Result: decisive resolution

Color Guidance
- Use a primary competitive accent for active states.
- Use distinct success, error, and neutral result colors.
- Avoid low-contrast decorative gradients behind the input area.

Shape Language
- Crisp cards, rounded rectangles, and letter badges.
- Avoid visual clutter and excessive bevel or skeuomorphism.
