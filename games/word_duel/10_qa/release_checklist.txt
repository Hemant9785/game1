Release Checklist

Gameplay Integrity
- Rules match documentation.
- Dictionary policy is consistent across services.
- Impossible-pair safeguards are active.
- Direction reveal is hidden before round start and synchronized correctly at round start.
- Simultaneous submit resolution is deterministic.

UX Readiness
- All critical messages are present and readable.
- Result, draw, and disconnect states are unambiguous.
- Rematch flow is fast and stable.

Technical Readiness
- Validation timeouts handled safely.
- Reconnect and disconnect logic tested.
- Duplicate submission protection tested.
- Telemetry captures key fairness and failure events.

Content Safety
- Offensive word filter active.
- Proper noun and abbreviation policy verified.

Store Readiness
- App icon and screenshots align with actual gameplay.
- Privacy disclosures reflect multiplayer and dictionary service behavior.
