Target Player

Primary Audience
- Mobile players aged roughly 13+ who enjoy fast puzzle, trivia, or word games.
- Players comfortable with light competition and quick rematches.

Player Traits
- Enjoys testing vocabulary without wanting long study-heavy gameplay.
- Prefers sessions under 5 minutes.
- Values fairness and clarity more than deep complexity.

Primary Motivations
- Beat another player in real time.
- Feel smart through fast word recall.
- Improve personal speed and consistency.
- Build a visible profile history through wins, streaks, and levels.

Secondary Audience
- Casual players who like simple games with low onboarding friction.
- Friends looking for short live challenges.

Potential Friction
- Very weak typists may feel disadvantaged.
- Players with limited English vocabulary may churn if letters feel unfair.
- Highly competitive players may demand stronger anti-cheat and ranked integrity.

Product Response
- Keep rules predictable.
- Provide strong invalid-input feedback.
- Avoid impossible letter pairs in normal matchmaking.
- Add progression rewards that acknowledge improvement, not only wins.
