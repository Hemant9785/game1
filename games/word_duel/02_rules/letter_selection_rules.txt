Letter Selection Rules

Selection Model
- Each player selects one letter during the pre-round phase.
- The pre-round phase does not reveal direction.
- At round start, the system randomly chooses one of two valid mappings:
- Option 1: Player 1 letter is the required first letter and Player 2 letter is the required last letter.
- Option 2: Player 2 letter is the required first letter and Player 1 letter is the required last letter.
- Both players see the final mapping only after the round begins.

Allowed Letters
- Standard English alphabet A-Z.

Selection Locking
- Once both players confirm, letters are locked for the round.
- If one player confirms first, their choice remains visible but cannot be changed unless the system explicitly allows an unlock countdown. MVP should not allow unlock to avoid ambiguity.
- After both letters lock, direction is assigned server-side and must not be knowable to either player in advance.

Timeout During Selection
- Recommended selection timer: 10 seconds.
- If a player does not choose in time, the system auto-assigns a letter using the fairness pool.

Fairness Restrictions
- Normal matchmaking should avoid impossible or extremely low-probability letter pairs.
- Letters such as Q, X, Z, J may be limited by matchmaking weighting, not fully removed.
- A blacklist of impossible pairings should be maintained if dictionary coverage proves them unwinnable.

Visibility
- Both selected letters should be shown before gameplay begins.
- Before round start, the UI should show the two chosen letters without labeling them as start or end.
- When gameplay begins, the final pair format should become explicit, for example: Start C / End T.

Future Variation
- Later modes may vary how letters are selected, but MVP should keep the letters visible and only hide direction.
