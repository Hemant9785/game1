Penalties

Invalid Submission Response
- Invalid words do not end the round.
- The submitting player receives immediate reason-based feedback.

Recommended Invalid Reasons
- Wrong starting letter
- Wrong ending letter
- Not found in dictionary
- Not allowed content
- Already tried
- Too short
- Invalid characters

Spam Control
- Repeated submissions faster than a minimum interval should trigger soft rate limiting.
- Recommended minimum submit interval: 400 to 600 ms.

Escalating Penalty Model
- First few invalid submissions: feedback only.
- Continued high-frequency invalid spam: short temporary submit cooldown of 1 to 2 seconds.
- Extreme bot-like behavior: flag account/session for anti-cheat review.

Disconnect Penalty
- A player who disconnects and fails to return within the grace window loses the round.

Abandon Penalty
- Manual quit counts as loss.
- Repeated abuse-driven quits may reduce sportsmanship score if that system exists later.

Penalty Design Goal
- Prevent abuse without turning ordinary mistakes into harsh punishment.
