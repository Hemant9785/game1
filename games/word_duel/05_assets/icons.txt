Icons

Required Icons
- Matchmaking/search
- Connection status
- Confirm/locked
- Warning/error
- Rematch
- Exit/back
- Trophy or win marker
- Draw marker

Style Rules
- Geometric and clean.
- Consistent stroke or fill family.
- Avoid overly playful iconography that conflicts with competitive tone.

Usage Notes
- Icons should support text, not replace it for critical states.
- Connection quality icon should be understandable at small size.
