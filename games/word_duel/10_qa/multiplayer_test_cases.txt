Multiplayer Test Cases

Core Multiplayer Cases
1. Both players connect normally and complete a round.
2. Both players see the same revealed direction at the same round-start moment.
3. One player disconnects during letter selection and fails to return.
4. One player disconnects during active round and reconnects within grace window.
5. One player disconnects during active round and does not return.
6. Both players submit valid words nearly simultaneously.
7. One player submits invalid spam rapidly; confirm rate limiting.
8. Rematch accepted by both players.
9. Rematch accepted by only one player.

Verification Focus
- Authoritative winner is consistent on both devices.
- State transitions match the documented match states.
- Reconnect restores the correct timer and letters.
