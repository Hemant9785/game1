Anti-Cheat

MVP Anti-Cheat Goals
- Preserve fair timing competition.
- Deter automated spam and client manipulation.

Threats
- Scripted rapid submissions
- Modified client bypassing paste restriction
- Spoofed timestamps
- Word-list brute force automation

MVP Protections
- Server-authoritative timing and validation
- Rate limiting on submission frequency
- Reject impossible character payloads
- Validate all final decisions server-side
- Log suspicious submission speed patterns

Signals For Review
- Extremely low reaction times repeatedly
- Repeated rapid invalid submissions at machine-like intervals
- Identical behavior across many matches

Product Stance
- False positives are costly. MVP should focus on detection and moderation hooks rather than aggressive auto-bans.
