Accessibility

Accessibility Goals
- Ensure the game remains usable under time pressure for a wide range of players.

Visual Accessibility
- Maintain strong contrast between letters, timer, and background.
- Do not rely on color alone to show valid/invalid status.
- Use readable font sizes for the core letters and timer.

Motor Accessibility
- Large touch targets for letter selection and submit.
- Avoid tiny dismiss buttons near primary actions.
- Permit keyboard-driven correction without punishing accidental mistypes more than necessary.

Reading Accessibility
- Use plain language in feedback.
- Keep status text short and persistent long enough to read.

Audio/Haptic Accessibility
- Important round events should not depend only on sound or only on haptics.
- Provide visual equivalents for all important feedback.

Fairness Note
- Because typing speed is central to the game, accessibility settings should improve readability and control clarity but should not automate submissions or bypass core competitive skill.
