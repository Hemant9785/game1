Error States

Error Handling Principles
- Tell the player what happened.
- Tell the player whether they can recover now.
- Avoid technical jargon unless no better wording exists.

Common Error States
- Network temporarily unavailable
- Match service unavailable
- Dictionary lookup timed out
- Opponent disconnected
- Submission cooldown active
- Validation service delayed

Required Player Guidance
- Retry now
- Wait while reconnecting
- Return to lobby
- Round decided due to disconnect

Dictionary Delay Messaging
- If dictionary validation is slow, show a short pending state instead of forcing players to wonder if the tap failed.
- If the delay exceeds an acceptable threshold, show "Still checking word" rather than surfacing API details.

Fatal Errors
- If the round cannot be resolved fairly, cancel the round and mark no contest rather than assigning a random loss.
