Reconnect Rules

Reconnect Goal
- Allow brief network drops without unfair instant losses.

Grace Window
- Recommended reconnect grace window: 10 seconds during active round.
- Recommended reconnect grace window: 15 seconds during non-active states such as rematch or lobby.

Behavior During Reconnect
- The disconnected player sees reconnecting status if possible.
- The connected opponent sees that the other player is reconnecting.
- The round timer continues during active round; the game does not pause for one player.

If Reconnect Succeeds
- Player receives the latest authoritative snapshot.
- Input can resume if the round is still active.

If Reconnect Fails
- Apply disconnect loss rules.

Fairness Note
- Reconnecting must not replay expired time or undo a winner already locked.
