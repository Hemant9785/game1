First-Time Flow

Goal
- Get a new player into a meaningful first duel in under 2 minutes.

Recommended Flow
1. Launch game.
2. See one-screen explanation that each player picks one letter and the game reveals start/end direction at round start.
3. Complete a short guided practice or visual sample.
4. Enter first live or bot-backed onboarding match.
5. See result and rematch prompt.

First Session Priorities
- Teach rule pattern before teaching competition depth.
- Prevent impossible letters in the very first experience.
- Keep wording plain and low jargon.

Must-Teach Concepts
- Your word must start with one revealed letter and end with the other revealed letter.
- The start/end direction is hidden until the round begins.
- Fastest valid word wins.
- Invalid words do not end the round.

Avoid In First-Time Flow
- Long account setup.
- Deep progression menus.
- Too many exceptions up front.
