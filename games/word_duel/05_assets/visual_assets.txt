Visual Assets

MVP Asset Needs
- App icon
- Word Duel logo wordmark
- Background treatment for lobby and gameplay
- Letter tile or letter badge components
- Result state badges for win, loss, draw
- Simple loader/pending indicator

Style Direction
- Minimalist and competitive.
- Clean typography with sharp contrast.
- Subtle energy rather than fantasy ornament.

Asset Priority
- Gameplay readability assets first.
- Brand polish assets second.

Reusable Components
- Start-letter badge
- End-letter badge
- Timer container
- Status chips
- Result medal or banner

Production Guidance
- Keep asset count low in MVP.
- Prefer scalable assets and reusable shape language.
