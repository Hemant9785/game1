# Word Duel Privacy Policy Template

Word Duel does not require user accounts. The app collects only the data needed to operate the gameplay experience.

## Data handled

- Player-entered display names, stored locally on device only during normal app use
- Player-entered words sent to the configured dictionary validation service
- Basic technical logs generated by the hosting platform for reliability and abuse prevention

## Data sharing

- Submitted words are sent to the configured dictionary validation endpoint so the app can verify whether a word is valid
- No advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, contacts, camera, microphone, location, or background tracking are included in this build

## Retention

- The app itself does not persist player profiles or gameplay history to a backend
- Hosting logs are retained according to your cloud provider policy
