Fonts

Font Requirements
- Highly readable for fast scanning.
- Distinct uppercase letterforms.
- Good numeral clarity for countdown timer.

Recommended Structure
- One strong display font for title and large letters.
- One simple UI font for controls and feedback.

Usage Rules
- Required letters should be visually bold and unmistakable.
- Feedback text should avoid stylized forms that slow reading.

Localization Consideration
- Even if MVP is English-only, choose fonts that will not block future expansion.
