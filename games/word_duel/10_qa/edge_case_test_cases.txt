Edge Case Test Cases

Cases
1. Submit at the exact final second.
2. Timer reaches zero before dictionary response returns.
3. Duplicate network delivery of same submission.
4. Opponent wins while local client still shows checking state.
5. Auto-assigned letters create no valid solution due to source bug.
6. App goes to background during active round.
7. App resumes after temporary network loss.
8. Keyboard paste attempt during gameplay.
9. Device rotation during selection and during active round.
10. Match cancelled because validation provider is unavailable too long.

Expected Outcome
- No silent failures.
- No contradictory winner displays.
- No implementation-specific leak of backend error details to players.
